cpp_M10_gate_60s_local.log is output of an earlier local harness for the single-file fallback engine.
Its per-case seconds are NOT a measure of the 60 s cap: it lists PILOT at 786.8 s and DFL001 at 895.9 s,
which exceed the cap, so the timer in that run did not enforce or measure the cap the way the current
benchmark does. "GATE GREEN" in that log means only that its pass/lost/wrong checks were met.
Do not cite this log as evidence of 60 s runtimes. The current evidence is benchmarks/netlib_gate.py with
the ledgers under results/ (per-case wall time, enforced cap, independent recheck).
